# UU1 manual outline

Source: `/home/research/old-games/game-uu1/Ultima_Underworld-Manual.pdf` (the
31-page Player's Guide; printed pp. 1–32 with p. 31 absent from the scan).
Citations are printed page numbers. Anything tagged [inference] is extrapolation,
not manual text.

## 1. Premise

- The Avatar, man or woman; gender affects portraits only, not stats (p. 2).
- The Avatar begins locked inside the Abyss behind huge double doors, in a dark
  entrance corridor with a bag, a skull, and a wall carving (pp. 4–5).
- The Abyss is a volcano; expect lava flows (pp. 20–21).
- The Abyss is divided into levels "in much the same way a building is divided
  into stories"; level 1 is uppermost (p. 19; the automap's dog-ears switch
  levels, p. 7).
- [inference — NOT in this manual's text]: the "8 levels" count and the full
  plot come from box copy and in-game dialogue, not this guide. Its only plot
  hints are dialogue picks ("rescue an innocent girl", "thrown in here as
  punishment for a crime I did not commit," p. 14).
- Structure is non-linear: move freely level to level, solve puzzles and quests
  in any order (p. 15).

## 2. Character creation (pp. 2, 18, 30)

Ordered choices (mouse or cursor-keys + ENTER): sex (portraits only),
handedness (weapon vs shield hand — screen-right is the figure's right, p. 6),
class, bonus skills, portrait (1 of 5), difficulty (Standard/Easy; locked after
start), name, keep-or-restart (p. 2).

- Classes (8): Fighter, Mage, Bard, Tinker, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Shepherd
  (one-line archetypes, p. 2).
- Attributes: ST, DX, INT; PC range 12–30; each class has its own
  high/medium/low profile (p. 18).
- Skills (20, each tagged with a governing attribute, p. 30): Acrobat,
  Appraise, Attack, Axe, Casting, Charm, Defense, Lore, Mace, Mana, Missile,
  Picklock, Repair, Search, Sneak, Swimming, Sword, Track, Traps, Unarmed. Each
  class grants required skills automatically plus a limited pick (p. 2).

## 3. Movement and 3D view (pp. 3–5, 20–21)

- Main screen: center first-person View Window, right Character Panel, bottom
  Message Scroll, six left Command Icons (Options/Talk/Get/Look/Fight/Use),
  plus Compass, Rune Shelf, Power Gem, Vitality/Mana Flasks, Current Spells
  (p. 3).
- Mouse move/look: cursor shape in the View Window sets direction and speed;
  hold LEFT to move. Compass spins with a red north tip. Head tilt on `1`/`2`/
  `3`. Jump is RIGHT-click while moving (no jumping in Fight mode); running
  speed at takeoff sets distance. Slow walk-off over a precipice is refused —
  leap it fast. Climb ≤2 ft by walking; higher needs jump or fly (pp. 4–5, 20).
- Easy Move Mode (accessibility): compass-arrow clicks turn 45° / step forward
  4 ft; `X` steps back 2 ft (p. 5).
- Swimming is automatic on entering water with the same controls but no jump
  or attack; exit only at banks ≤2 ft above water; the drown timer follows
  Swimming skill + encumbrance; dropped items sink irretrievably (p. 21).
- Flying via spell or item (`E` ascend, `Q` descend/land, p. 21). Lava is
  walkable but damaging — leap it (p. 21).

## 4. Command icons, Default Mode, Options (pp. 5, 16–17)

- One icon active at a time; RIGHT button performs the active verb, LEFT moves
  and drags. Default Mode (no icon lit): RIGHT-click = Look, RIGHT-drag =
  Get-or-Use auto-detect, brief RIGHT-drag on a person = Talk, LEFT-click on an
  inventory item = Use, click on the weapon-hand weapon = toggle Fight (p. 16).
- Options pauses: Save/Restore (slots I–IV + typed description), Music/Sound
  toggles, Detail (Very High→Low), Return to Game, Quit to DOS (pp. 16–17).

## 5. Combat (pp. 9, 13–14, 22–23)

- Fight mode shows the weapon tip; the weapon MUST be in the weapon-hand
  circle. Aim at the foe's middle (no hit locations); press-hold RIGHT to draw
  back, release to strike. Aim locks at press, not release.
- Power Gem: red = aborted fail, yellow = quick weak hit, green sparkling =
  full strength (pp. 13, 22).
- Swing by press position: top third = overhead bash, middle = sideways slash,
  bottom = thrust; damage varies per weapon; unarmed = jab only; each missile
  type has one attack form. Small foes need middle/low or look-down swings.
- Feedback: blood splatter size = damage; the view rocks when the avatar is
  hit, full-red flash = serious; foe condition reads on the gargoyle eyes
  above the view (green/yellow/red, p. 22).
- XP for kills; loot drops to the ground (p. 14).
- Missiles: weapon in hand + matching ammo in inventory; press position
  irrelevant; release at green with no bonus for holding longer; lead moving
  targets; some ammo breaks, some is recoverable (p. 22).
- Locked doors and chests can be battered down by repeated attack — slow, and
  it may damage or destroy the weapon (pp. 9, 22).
- Weapon families (p. 23): Swords (dagger/short/long/broad), Axes
  (hand/pole/battle), Maces (cudgel/light/mace), Missiles (bow/crossbow/sling;
  sling bullets are weak and easy — make them by breaking boulders). Bigger =
  more damage, slower ready.
- Armor: leather < chain < plate; mix-and-match per slot; degrades until
  repaired or destroyed; plate is heavy. Shields work ONLY in the off-hand
  circle. Item condition shows on Look (pp. 6, 23).

## 6. Inventory, paper doll, weight (pp. 6–8, 10, 14, 18, 21)

- Character Panel: portrait ringed by wear/hold circles + bottom inventory
  circles; chain flips to Statistics, then to the Rune Panel when the rune bag
  is open (pp. 11, 18).
- Wear slots: weapon-hand circle, off-hand circle, 2 shoulder slots
  (torch/map), head/torso/hands/legs/feet on the portrait, 2 finger slots
  (rings work ONLY when worn, p. 21).
- Carry capacity in stones on the gray circle; 0 = drop first. Encumbrance
  slows movement, shortens jumps and swim time (pp. 18, 21).
- Drag with RIGHT-press-hold; drop = release in the bottom third of the view,
  throw = top two-thirds; blocked throws fall at the feet. Fragiles can break
  (p. 8).
- Stacks need identical type AND quality; grouped pickup prompts "Move how
  many?" (LEFT = one, RIGHT = all, or type a number, p. 10).
- Bags carry organized kits and nest; rune stones never leave the rune bag once
  in (pp. 10–11, 21). Items combine by drag (torch + corn = popcorn, p. 21).

## 7. Magic: runes, preparation, casting (pp. 10–11, 26–29, 32)

- Anyone with runes + bag can attempt; success scales with Casting skill, Mana,
  and character level (pp. 11, 26).
- Preparation: open the rune bag (alphabetical panel, blanks = missing), place
  stones on the Rune Shelf; the shelf persists — pre-buff before fights. The
  bottom symbol clears it (pp. 11, 27).
- Casting: LEFT-click the shelf runes; failures cost no Mana (only time) except
  rare backfires that wound low-Casting casters (pp. 11, 26).
- Gates (p. 26): Mana cost = 3 × Circle; character level/2 rounded up must meet
  the Circle; recast delay scales with level + Circle.
- Durations: icons left of the compass; max 3 concurrent; LEFT-click dispels,
  RIGHT-click names + stable/unstable time. Spells fade, e.g. over sleep
  (pp. 19, 27).
- Targeted casting via RIGHT-click on the shelf: red circle = combat, blue
  cross = utility; needs clear space ahead; blocked point-blank fails. Area
  spells vary and can destroy nearby valuables (p. 27).
- Enchanted items bypass level/Mana: wearables act while worn, scrolls act on
  Use; all look mundane until Lore identifies them on Look (p. 27).
- Grimoire (pp. 28–29): 8 Circles × 5 spells (40 total) with rune formulas and
  behaviors, from Create Food/Light/Magic Arrow/Resist Blows/Stealth (1st)
  through Fly/Invisibility (7th) to Flame Wind/Freeze Time/Iron Flesh/Roaming
  Sight/Tremor (8th). 24 runes (p. 32): AN BET CORP DES EX FLAM GRAV HUR IN
  JUX KAL LOR MANI NOX ORT POR QUAS REL SANCT TYM UUS VAS WIS YLEM.

## 8. Conversation, NPCs, barter, repair (pp. 14–15, 24–25)

- Talk icon + RIGHT-click; most humanoids and some creatures; usually
  impossible mid-fight. "When in doubt, talk first" (p. 24).
- Screen: portrait + name, NPC dialogue on the large scroll, player options on
  the message scroll, dual barter areas, [MORE] paging, per-NPC farewell line.
- NPCs remember treatment; attitude varies; anger can turn violent. Some
  prompts accept free text (p. 24).
- Barter: goods into the barter areas, light/unlight trade dots, "I offer you
  this trade" to propose; accept swaps, decline lets you rebid. Same UI for
  gifts and showing items (p. 25).
- "I must think about this deal" appraises scaled by Appraise; traders differ
  in patience, profit demand, valuations (p. 25). Demands seize by threat —
  always hostile (p. 25).
- Repair via NPC (broken item in the avatar's barter area first) or self at an
  anvil (difficulty prompt; Repair governs; failure can RUIN the item; slow +
  noisy, p. 25).

## 9. Automap and notes (pp. 6–8)

Square parchment pickup; explored walls auto-draw in brown ink + yellow pushpin
self-marker. LEFT-click opens fullscreen; quill writes typed notes at the click
point; eraser deletes; dog-ear corners flip mapped levels; Close/Esc exits.

## 10. Survival: food, light, sleep, poison, XP/leveling (pp. 6, 10, 14, 18–20)

- RIGHT-click the compass for fed/rested state + level depth + day/time (p. 19).
- Hunger → eat via Use or drag-to-mouth; Create Food exists (1st Circle).
- Sleep on `F10` or Use a bedroll (deeper rest); restores Vitality + Mana, less
  if hungry or near lairs (ambush risk); douse lights first (pp. 14, 19).
- Light: torches brightest, candles dimmer but longer-lived; condition matters;
  Night Vision/Daylight/Light spells substitute (pp. 6, 10–11, 28–29).
- Poison turns the red flask bright green (spoiled food, poisoned weapons,
  potions, diseased bites); slow Vitality drain; antidote potion cures, and the
  manual hints at further solutions (p. 19).
- Flasks: red = Vitality, blue = Mana; click for exact numbers (p. 19).
- XP for exploring, kills, heroic deeds (lost for unheroic acts); level-ups to
  max 16 are automatic — raising Vitality + unlocking Circles (pp. 18–20).
  Skill-ups are MANUAL at ankh shrines: Talk, chant a mantra at "Chant the
  Mantra:"; rejected unless ready. Known mantras: Summ Ra (attacks), Mu Ahm
  (magic), Om Cah (misc); hidden dungeon mantras grant big single-skill gains
  (p. 20).

## 11. Saving, death, resurrection (pp. 4, 12, 14, 17)

- Save early and often: Options → Save Game → slot I–IV → typed description;
  blocked while an item is in hand or mid-action. Restore via Options or the
  title's "Journey Onward" (p. 17).
- The tutorial route chains the entry doors, weapon and candle bags, rune
  finds, the block-room pack (scroll + red key + rune bag + runes), the south
  door, and the Silver Sapling: harvest a seed, plant it with Use on dirt with
  clear floor ahead (pp. 8–12).
- The planted seed is the respawn anchor: death resurrects there; without one,
  death returns to the title screen (pp. 12, 14). Secret doors: some visible,
  most need Look-at-wall + Search; NPC hints help (p. 12).
